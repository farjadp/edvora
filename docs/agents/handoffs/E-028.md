3. **Decisions made:**
   - **Ledger as source of truth:** totals are a cache, rebuilt from the ledger, and corrections are `reversal` rows, so XP is auditable and recomputable (ADR 014).
   - **Deferred hooks:** gamification writes made from collection hooks wait until the caller's transaction ends. A second connection deadlocked in testing.
   - **Daily cap scope:** the cap covers only the student's own actions. Milestones, the weekly bonus and the grade bonus are exempt.
   - **Levels:** levels 1–5 come from peak XP. Level 6 (پیشگام) is the issued certificate. XP never unlocks a certificate.
   - **Streaks per enrollment,** because drip is per course or cohort. A neutral week is one with nothing released, or one that falls in a break.
   - **The activity ledger always records,** so switching gamification back on restores everything.
   - **Advisor goal:** it counts distinct leads with a recorded outcome, and moving only the date logs `date_moved`, which doesn't count.
   - **Dark-mode brand text** uses the new per-tenant `--brand-text` token, which is ≥ 4.5:1 for any tenant colour (fix for QA-028-01).
   - **PRD placeholders use defaults as tenant config:** Saturday week start, 2 days a week, Thu/Fri staff days off, leaderboard minimum 8, level thresholds, badge names.
