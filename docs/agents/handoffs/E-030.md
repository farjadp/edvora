## Output
- **Importer:** `pnpm --filter @edvora/web import:iba` → `apps/web/src/seed/import-iba.ts` + `seed/iba/`:
  `http.ts` (polite, cached fetcher; honours robots.txt; ≥1.5 s between requests; cache in git-ignored
  `apps/web/.cache/iba-import`), `extract.ts` (testimonials, logos, alumni and posts from Elementor HTML / WP REST),
  `lexical.ts` (HTML → Payload rich text, images into Media), `curated.json` + `curated.ts` (course/page text
  transcribed verbatim, Zod-validated). It is idempotent (re-run gives identical row counts) and works offline from
  the cache (`IBA_IMPORT_OFFLINE=1`). It writes a per-item report to `apps/web/.cache/iba-import/report.json`.
- **Imported locally:** 20 courses imported (9 published; 11 past or undescribed workshops kept as drafts) — the `iba` catalogue now shows
  14 published courses including the seed's 3 graduation bundles and «برندسازی شخصی», 10 faculty with photos, 21 testimonials (photo, quote, video link), 28 published journal posts with
  covers and inline images, 5 published pages (about, FAQ, contact, regulations, consulting) + 2 drafts (founder,
  careers), 24 client logos, academy logo, contact address. 129 images self-hosted in Media, each with alt text and
  `sourceUrl`. Nothing is hotlinked.
- **Public pages added:** `/journal`, `/journal/<slug>`, `/faculty`, `/<page-slug>` (content pages, served by E-032's
  catch-all `[...wpPath]`: a published page first, then an imported WordPress redirect, then 404); menu and
  footer list published pages; home shows logo images, testimonial photos, journal covers; course pages show
  faculty photos.
- **Schema (migration `20261106_224247_e030_iba_import`, small):** `sourceUrl` on courses/posts/testimonials/media/
  pages; `courses.pendingClaims` (staff-only read, never rendered); tenant-owned `pages`; `tenants.contact.address`;
  `testimonials.consentNote` (staff-only) and `sortOrder`. Access tightened: anonymous API sees only published pages,
  posts with a past `publishedAt`, and approved testimonials.
- **Text rules:** `cleanPersianText` in `packages/core/src/persian.ts` (+ tests): ی/ک, kashida, half-spaces, Persian
  digits in prose, «٬» thousands, RLM after «)» closing Latin text (fixes flipped «(Brand MBA)»).
- **Demo access:** `src/proxy.ts` + `packages/core/src/previewGate.ts` (+ tests): `PRIVATE_PREVIEW_HOSTS` get
  `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, optional basic auth (`PRIVATE_PREVIEW_USER/PASSWORD`). `MEDIA_DIR` env for persistent
  media. Importer remote mode uploads images through the deployed app's API. Runbook: *IBA client preview (E-030)* in
  `docs/runbooks/deploy-railway.md`. **Not run against Railway.**
- **Docs:** `docs/content/iba-inventory.md` (URL → collection/field mapping, what has no home);
  `.claude/agents/content-producer.md` + roster entry.
- **Screenshots** (before | after, desktop 1440 and mobile 390): `docs/agents/handoffs/E-030/*.jpg` — home, Brand MBA,
  Brand DBA, faculty, testimonials, journal, an article.
- Checks: `pnpm typecheck` ✓, `pnpm test` ✓ (core 154, ui 17, integrations 6), `pnpm build` ✓. Leak check: no
  «وزارت»/«قابل ترجمه» on any public page or anonymous REST response; drafts and consent notes not exposed.

## Decisions made
- **Coordinated with E-032 (#19):** its WordPress importer now writes `sourceUrl` to courses, posts and media (the
  E-030 column) and writes WordPress pages into E-030 `pages` (HTML → Lexical body, excerpt → intro, Persian slugs
  allowed); its post redirects point at `/journal/<slug>` (there is no `/blog`). No duplicate fields.
- **Prices not imported.** Every price on the site is tied to a past cohort or contradicts another page (e.g. MBA
  35,770,000; DBA 66,600,000 cash / 77,700,000 instalments; Premium the same 66.6M). Courses keep `price: 0` →
  «تماس بگیرید». The seed's graduation-bundle prices (current, from Notion 02) are untouched. TODO(client).
- **Stale dates not shown.** MBA (start بهمن ۱۴۰۳) and DBA (آبان ۱۴۰۴) are published without dates; past workshops
  are drafts. Running courses keep the seed's cohorts (Strategic Thinking, Premium).
- **MBA/DBA always published** (core offer); workshops published only if current and described.
- **Testimonials shown** (`approvedForUse: true`) because the brief asked for them in the demo and they are public on
  the client's site today — each carries a `consentNote` to reconfirm. The PM review recommended `false` until
  consent; that is Farjad's call (one click per row in admin, or tell me to flip them).
- **Videos linked, not copied.** The 12 testimonial videos stay on the academy's own ArvanCloud VOD
  (`brandgancom.arvanvod.ir`), opened on click.
- **Founder résumé and careers pages are drafts.** Résumé holds unverified official/government roles; careers form
  asks for a face photo.
- **Railway preview approved by Farjad (2026-11-06) as an ADR 002 exception** (recorded in the ADR). Railway gives
  one generated domain per service, so it runs as a second service `web-iba` (same code and DB, own volume) at
  https://web-iba-production.up.railway.app — `noindex`, basic auth, fails closed until the password is set.
  Deployed from this branch; `web` (demo) untouched. Remove `web-iba` after the meeting.
