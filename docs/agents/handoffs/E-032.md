3. **Decisions made**
   - **Plugin first, REST fallback, WXR for posts and media only, SQL dump deferred.** Only the plugin reaches phones
     in usermeta, LMS custom tables and theme meta on cheap hosts.
   - **Gzip NDJSON bundle, not zip.** `ZipArchive` is often missing on Iranian shared hosts.
   - **Mapping lives in TypeScript; the plugin stays thin.** Fixes ship server-side and get re-run without
     customers updating the plugin.
   - **No password hashes are imported.** Students log in by OTP and staff re-onboard with TOTP. Verifying WP hashes
     is possible but isn't needed.
   - **Users are keyed by normalised phone.**
     - Duplicate phones, and users with no phone, are held for review; they are never merged silently.
     - A user who already exists in another academy only gains membership in this one.
     - The WP email goes to `contactEmail`, never to the login email.
   - **WooCommerce prices win over LMS plan prices.** At IBA the LifterLMS plan price is a placeholder `1`.
   - **Imported courses are drafts.** The academy publishes them.
   - **Orders:** completed, processing and refunded are imported; the rest are counted. One WP order with two courses
     becomes two Edvora orders.
   - **Idempotent re-runs by refs and checksums.** Records edited in Edvora become conflicts and are not
     overwritten. Nothing is ever deleted.
   - **The connection secret** is shown once and stored as a hash plus a sealed copy. It is bound to one tenant and
     one job, expires after 72 h and is revoked when the job ends. Requests are HMAC-signed, with a timestamp,
     nonce and rate limit.
   - **Redirects use 308**, which Next's `permanentRedirect` sends and search engines treat as permanent.
